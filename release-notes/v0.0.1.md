First release of Oru, an Android app that patches other apps on your own phone.

- Simple and Expert. In Simple, each app shows one sentence and one button. Expert lets you choose the version, patches, options, installer and patch source for each app.
- One progress for the whole run, with the real stages and counts, shown as a hexagon that fills as your app is patched.
- Everything happens on the phone. Oru has no accounts and no analytics, and it does not upload your APK files.
- Patched apps are signed with a key that is created on your phone at the first patch. Back it up from Settings, Advanced.
- You choose the patch source for each app. Adding a source never changes the apps you already set up.
- Oru checks for new releases of itself and verifies the download against `SHA256SUMS.txt` and the installed signature before it asks Android to install it.

Check the download against `SHA256SUMS.txt` on this release before you install it. The speed measurement and its limits are in `docs/benchmarks.md`.
