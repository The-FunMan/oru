# Code of Conduct

This project follows the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1. The full text is at
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html and is licensed under CC BY 4.0.

## Our standard

We want participation in Oru to be welcoming and harassment-free for everyone. In short:

- be respectful and constructive in issues, pull requests and discussions;
- assume good faith, and say clearly when you disagree;
- do not harass, insult or threaten anyone, and do not publish their private information;
- keep discussions on the project's work.

## Reporting

To report a conduct concern, use GitHub's private reporting: open the **Security** tab of this repository and choose **Report a vulnerability**, and say in the report that it is a conduct concern. The maintainer reads these reports and responds on a best-effort basis.

## Enforcement

The maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits and other contributions that break this standard, and may limit or end a person's participation. Enforcement follows the Contributor Covenant 2.1 enforcement guidelines.
